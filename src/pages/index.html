02 · The rules we work under

Only with permission, only in scope.

We assess only systems the client owns or is authorized to control, under written authorization that names each asset and how far we may go. A passive review reads public records and sends no probing traffic. Active testing — anything that touches your systems — happens only inside a signed scope, in an agreed window. Hardening changes are made with you present, authorizing each one.

We do not do penetration testing or red-team work. When a weakness is confirmed, the job is to document it and close it, not to prove how far it could be taken.

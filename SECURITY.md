# Security policy

If you find a security problem with theclauditor.ai, with mail from theclauditor.ai, or with anything in this repository, please tell us.

## How to report

- Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository, or
- write to hello@theclauditor.ai. If you prefer to encrypt, our PGP key is at <https://theclauditor.ai/.well-known/pgp-key.txt>. The same details are published at <https://theclauditor.ai/.well-known/security.txt>.

## What you can expect

An acknowledgment within three business days. A plain-English reply about what we found and what we fixed. Credit, if you want it, once the problem is fixed.

## What we ask

Give us reasonable time to fix a problem before you publish it. Do not read, change or keep data that is not yours. No denial-of-service tests, no social engineering of people who work with us, no physical attempts.

## Safe harbor

If you follow this page in good faith, we consider your research authorized and will not pursue legal action over it. If a third party does, we will say so on your behalf.

## Scope

In scope: theclauditor.ai, www.theclauditor.ai, mta-sts.theclauditor.ai, mail sent from theclauditor.ai, and this repository. Out of scope: GitHub's, Cloudflare's and Google's own systems, which host the repository, the site and the mail — report those to them.
